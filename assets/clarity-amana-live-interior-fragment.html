⚖️ Inheritance (Farāʾiḍ) — from your family tree

💡 How to use Inheritance (Farāʾiḍ) · quick guide
  1. Mark the tree first — living heirs need gender and vital status on the Family Tree.
  2. Load heirs — pull living relatives into this calculator; confirm who is a Qurʾānic sharer (farḍ) vs residual (ʿaṣabah).
  3. Estate net — funeral/debts first; optional wasiyyah ≤ ⅓ to non-heirs; then fixed shares (4:11–12, 4:176).
  4. Review — check ʿawl / radd notes if totals exceed or fall short; print only as a study sheet.

Educational tool only — not a fatwa, not a court order. Schools may differ on rare cases; consult a qualified scholar for a real estate.

Educational tool only — not a fatwa, not legal advice. Hanafi study aid — other madhāhib differ; no binding distribution. Core shares follow Qurʾān 4:11–12 and 4:176 with a simplified Sunni (Hanafi-leaning) model including ʿawl and radd. Complex cases (grandfather with siblings, uterine heirs, ʿUmariyyatān, school differences, civil law) need a qualified scholar or local court. Debts and funeral costs come before shares; wasiyyah to a non-heir is capped at one-third of what remains after debts.

📜 Wasiyyah — Islamic will & bequest

💡 How to draft a Wasiyyah · quick guide
  1. Order of rights — burial/funeral costs and debts first; then optional bequests; then farāʾiḍ for heirs.
  2. ⅓ rule — optional wasiyyah to non-heirs / charity is capped at one-third of the net (unless heirs consent).
  3. Do not use bequests to increase a fixed-share heir’s portion beyond the Qurʾān.
  4. Guardians & executor (waṣī) — pick trusted adults from the tree or type names; name an alternate.
  5. Save / print — device-local draft only; formal wills need local legal + scholarly review.

Educational draft only — not a fatwa, not a notarized will. Align with Qurʾān 4:11–12 and local law; ask a scholar and, where required, a lawyer. Farāʾiḍ calculator above is a Hanafi study aid — other madhāhib differ; no binding distribution.

Draft a wasiyyah guided by Sunni fiqh: pay debts & funeral first, optional bequests up to ⅓ of the remainder to non-heirs, then the rest follows farāʾiḍ. This is an educational worksheet — not a lawyer-certified document. Finalise with a qualified scholar and local solicitor/notary for your country.

Fiqh core (consensus): Net estate = assets − funeral − debts. Optional wasiyyah ≤ ⅓ of net to people or causes who are not fixed-share heirs (Prophet ﷺ: “no bequest to an heir”). Excess over ⅓ needs heir consent after death. Document debts, trusts, and guardians clearly.
