Privacy & on-device data

Educational overview — not a legal contract and not legal advice.

Local-first

Amānah-related notes and similar tools are designed so sensitive material stays on your device where implemented that way. Clarity is not advertising a public feed of your private ledger.

What we aim not to do

  • Store your vault passphrase on Clarity servers
  • Publish private trust notes as public web pages

What you should still do

  • Use a strong device passcode
  • Back up anything important with a method you control
  • Treat educational screens as learning aids, not court filings
