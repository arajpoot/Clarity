What the sources say

The Prophet ﷺ encouraged remembering death so that it softens the next choice — not so that it freezes the living. Confirm wording with a teacher; browse related chapters on Sunnah.com.

How schools differ

Details of optional wasiyyah, Qur’an gifted to the dead, and janāzah form vary. This hub does not pick a madhhab. Follow a local imam.

Common mistakes

  • Treating a website calculator as a court order
  • Writing a “will” that tries to cancel Qur’anic shares
  • Delaying debts because “the family will sort it”

FAQ

Is this a complete Islamic preparation course?

No. It is a one-week study map. Ask a scholar for your case.

Do I need a lawyer?

If you live under civil probate (UK, Canada, much of the world), usually yes for a valid civil will — plus a scholar so it does not fight farāʾiḍ.

Can I skip days?

Yes. The order is pedagogical, not a ritual.
