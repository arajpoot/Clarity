Prepare for death

Remembering death is not meant to freeze the heart — it softens it toward repentance, prayer, and kindness.

Use this page with the Seeker and Daily paths in the app: Grave Path, Night Breath, and Commands.

Learning aims

  • Understand that every soul will taste death.
  • Turn remembrance into action: ṣalāh, ṣadaqah, restoring rights.
  • Seek knowledge of janazah and wasiyyah without panic.
