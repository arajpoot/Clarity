Duʿāʾ for the deceased

Supplicating for forgiveness and mercy is a gift the living can offer. Use authentic wordings taught by reliable teachers.

Educational only — not a fatwa. Wordings and practice details can vary. Confirm with a qualified local teacher. Acceptance is with Allah. Prefer primary pages on Quran.com and Sunnah.com.

Sourced learning panels

1. Janāzah duʿāʾ theme — forgiveness and mercy

اللَّهُمَّ اغْفِرْ لَهُ وَارْحَمْهُ

O Allah, forgive him and have mercy on him.

For a woman, teachers often adjust endings (e.g. lahā / irḥamhā). Longer forms are reported from the Prophet ﷺ in the funeral prayer context.

Source to verify: Ṣaḥīḥ Muslim · 963 · sunnah.com/muslim:963 — open the full report; do not treat this short line as the entire narration.

2. On hearing of a loss

إِنَّا لِلَّهِ وَإِنَّا إِلَيْهِ رَاجِعُونَ

Indeed we belong to Allah, and indeed to Him we will return.

Qur’ān 2:156 · quran.com/2/156

Fuller prophetic teaching for the one afflicted is also reported in the Sunnah — verify the exact wording and number on Sunnah.com with a teacher.

3. After burial — ask forgiveness and firmness

A report from ʿUthmān (may Allah be pleased with him) describes the Prophet ﷺ standing at the grave and instructing companions to seek forgiveness for their brother and ask that he be made firm, for he is being questioned.

Source to verify: Sunan Abī Dāwūd · 3221 · sunnah.com/abudawud:3221 — check grade and full text on the primary page.

4. What continues after death (hope for the living)

When a person dies, their deeds come to an end except three: ongoing charity, knowledge from which benefit is gained, and a righteous child who prays for them.

Ṣaḥīḥ Muslim · 1631 · sunnah.com/muslim:1631
