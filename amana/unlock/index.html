You are responsible for your passphrase. Clarity does not store it and cannot recover vault data if it is lost. On-device only · encrypted at rest · educational — not a fatwa.
What this vault is for

Amana Vault holds private notes for estate readiness: who you leave behind, debts and trusts to settle first, wasiyyah notes, family outline, and farāʾiḍ study tools. It is a trust (amānah) between you and those who may need this after you — not a court filing and not a fatwa.

Why do it the right way — Allah commands justice in what we leave behind. Harmful or unjust wills are a serious matter. The Prophet ﷺ warned against injustice in the will:

“Indeed a man and a woman perform deeds in obedience to Allah for sixty years, then death presents itself to them, and they cause such harm in the will that the Fire becomes warranted for them.” — reported from al-Tirmidhī · educational wording; verify full isnād and fiqh with a scholar

Use this space to organise names, rights, and notes so heirs are not left in confusion — then confirm inheritance and wasiyyah with a qualified local scholar and, where needed, counsel.
