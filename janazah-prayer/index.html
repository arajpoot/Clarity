Janazah prayer

Legacy path · Day 6 of 7 · ← Day 5 · Ghusl →

One sentence: stand if able, four takbīrs, no rukūʿ in the standard form, follow the imam.

Disclaimer. Educational outline. The living imam leads. Do not argue mid-prayer.

Last reviewed: September 2026 · funeral chapters on Sunnah.com

Do this today

  • Read the four-takbīr sequence once
  • Learn one short duʿāʾ for the deceased
  • At a real janāzah, follow the imam

Widely taught sequence

  1. Stand facing qiblah if able (no rukūʿ / sujūd in the standard form).
  2. 1st takbīr — then al-Fātiḥah (as many schools teach).
  3. 2nd takbīr — ṣalawāt upon the Prophet ﷺ.
  4. 3rd takbīr — duʿāʾ for the deceased.
  5. 4th takbīr — brief close as taught, then taslīm.

A short duʿāʾ

اللَّهُمَّ اغْفِرْ لَهُ وَارْحَمْهُ وَعَافِهِ وَاعْفُ عَنْهُ

O Allah, forgive him, have mercy on him, grant him well-being, and pardon him. Confirm full wording with your imam. More texts: duʿāʾ index.

How schools differ

  • Four takbīrs are standard in common teaching.
  • Raising the hands at each takbīr differs.
  • Whether followers recite Fātiḥah differs (Ḥanafī teaching often has the imam’s recitation suffice).
  • Wording of the third-takbīr duʿāʾ differs in length.

Do what the imam does.

Common mistakes

  • Bowing as in daily ṣalāh
  • Filming the row instead of praying
  • Correcting the imam loudly in the prayer
