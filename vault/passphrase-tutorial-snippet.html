How your passphrase protects this vault

Educational only — not a fatwa · not legal advice · not a court document.

  1. On this device only. Your passphrase unlocks Amānah Vault here. Clarity does not receive it.
  2. Choose a strong phrase you do not reuse elsewhere.
  3. Memorize or store offline. Clarity cannot reset a forgotten passphrase.
  4. Never type it into chat tools (Claude, Grok, email, screenshots).
  5. Export / email / API use snapshots built after unlock — not your passphrase.
  6. Heir readiness is a personal aid, not a legal document.
