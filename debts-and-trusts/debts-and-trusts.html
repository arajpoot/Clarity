Debts and trusts

Educational only — not a fatwa. Before farāʾiḍ and wasiyyah, clear what you owe and protect what you hold in trust (amānah).

One action today: notify or begin to clear one item this week.

What belongs on this list

  • Loans, unpaid wages, borrowed items
  • Apologies and rights between people still unsettled
  • Keys, documents, money, or secrets held for others
