Educational enrichment · what continues

Sūrat al-ʿAṣr frames the whole Yawm path: faith, righteous work, truth, and patience. Reports about continuing benefit after death (often cited from Muslim 1631) mention ongoing charity, knowledge others benefit from, and a righteous child who prays — confirm full wording and grade. Yawm does not replace teachers or local scholarship; it only points the heart to act while alive.

Sūrat al-ʿAṣr

وَالْعَصْرِ ۝ إِنَّ الْإِنسَانَ لَفِي خُسْرٍ ۝ إِلَّا الَّذِينَ آمَنُوا وَعَمِلُوا الصَّالِحَاتِ وَتَوَاصَوْا بِالْحَقِّ وَتَوَاصَوْا بِالصَّبْرِ

By time, mankind is in loss, except those who believe, do righteous deeds, and enjoin one another to truth and to patience.

Qur’an · 103:1–3 · educational meaning

Qur’an

Ongoing charity, knowledge, righteous child

When a person dies, his deeds come to an end except three: ongoing charity, knowledge by which people benefit, and a righteous child who prays for him.
