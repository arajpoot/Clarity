The first matter of accountability

The first matter concerning which a servant will be brought to account on the Day of Resurrection is the prayer. If it is sound, the rest of his deeds will be sound; if it is corrupt, the rest of his deeds will be corrupt.

Reported via al-Ṭabarānī and others; often graded ṣaḥīḥ or ḥasan by later scholars — confirm chain with a teacher. Educational reminder only.
