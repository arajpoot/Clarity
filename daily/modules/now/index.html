Educational enrichment · day shape

Many educational guides structure the day around the five prayers as anchors—not a rigid timetable. A common pattern:

  • Before / at Fajr — wake with dhikr, optional night prayer if able, Fajr sunnah then farḍ.
  • After Fajr — morning adhkār, a portion of Qur’an while the mind is fresh.
  • Through the day — work and family folded between Dhuhr, ʿAṣr, Maghrib, ʿIshāʾ.
  • Evening — evening adhkār, reduce harm, prepare sleep with remembrance.

Adjust everything to your local times and capacity. Educational only — not a fatwa.
