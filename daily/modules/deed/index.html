Educational enrichment · ongoing good

Public educational summaries of a well-known report (Muslim 1631 and similar) mention that when a person dies, their deeds end except continuous charity, knowledge that is benefited from, and a righteous child who prays for them. Yawm’s “one deed” is a practical mirror of that teaching: something small you can actually do today.

  • Teach or share one verified beneficial sentence.
  • Remove a harm from a path others use.
  • Pray one ṣalāh with more presence.
  • Seek forgiveness with attention of the heart.

Always confirm hadith wording and grade with a teacher. Acceptance is with Allah.

What continues after death

When a person dies, his deeds come to an end except three: ongoing charity, knowledge from which benefit is gained, and a righteous child who prays for him.
