Educational enrichment · what scholars compile

IslamQA and similar educational summaries collect ṣaḥīḥ reports for morning and evening remembrance, including:

  • Sayyid al-istighfār — Bukhārī 6306 (best form of seeking forgiveness — confirm full text).
  • Subḥān Allāh wa bi-ḥamdih — Muslim 2692, often 100 times morning and evening.
  • Protection phrases — e.g. “Bismillāh alladhī lā yaḍurru…” (Abū Dāwūd / Tirmidhī chains — verify grade).

Popular booklets such as Hisn al-Muslim (Fortress of the Muslim) arrange these for daily use. Prefer authentic chains; when grades differ, ask a teacher. Consistency matters more than rushing a long list.
