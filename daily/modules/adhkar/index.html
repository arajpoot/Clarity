Subḥān Allāh wa bi-ḥamdih

سُبْحَانَ اللَّهِ وَبِحَمْدِهِ

Whoever says in the morning and in the evening: “Glory and praise be to Allah” one hundred times, no one will come on the Day of Resurrection with anything better than what he has done — except one who said the same or more.

Ṣaḥīḥ Muslim · 2692 · educational summary

Sayyid al-istighfār

The best way of seeking forgiveness includes affirming Allah as Lord and asking forgiveness — whoever says it in the day with certainty and dies before evening is among the people of Paradise (and likewise for the night), as in the report.

Ṣaḥīḥ al-Bukhārī · 6306 · educational summary — read full Arabic with a teacher
