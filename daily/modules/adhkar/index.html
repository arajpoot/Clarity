Subḥān Allāh wa bi-ḥamdih

سُبْحَانَ اللَّهِ وَبِحَمْدِهِ

Whoever says this in the morning and evening one hundred times — no one will bring better than what he has done, except one who said the same or more.

Ṣaḥīḥ Muslim · 2692 · educational summary

Sayyid al-istighfār

The best form of seeking forgiveness is taught in Bukhārī 6306 (ṣaḥīḥ). Learn the full Arabic with a teacher.
