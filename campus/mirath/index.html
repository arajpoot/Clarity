Study syllabus (themes)
  1. Why inheritance is discussed in revelation — justice and family care.
  2. Categories of heirs in classical study (overview only).
  3. Debts, bequests, and limits often mentioned in textbooks.
  4. What continues after death in reports (e.g. beneficial knowledge, ongoing charity, righteous child’s duʿāʾ) — study texts carefully.
  5. Local law may apply; personal situations need a qualified teacher or counsel.
The text does not settle this for you

Muslim 1631 lists three things that continue from what the person left. Other reports discuss duʿāʾ and charity from the living. Ask a trusted teacher for your situation. This page does not issue a verdict.
