University Charter

Limits first. This is a private educational tool on your device — not a physical campus and not a ruling body.

What this is

  • A local-first learning path: Seeker → Practice → Consistency → Transmission
  • Practice halls (Lab): Arabic, Tajweed, Recite, Hifz, Calligraphy, Media
  • Study markers on this browser only — a mirror, not a scoreboard of īmān
  • Pointers to Qurʾān and Sunnah sources for further reading

What this is not

  • Not a physical university or accredited degree
  • Not affiliated with Al-Azhar, Madinah, IIUM, or any institution
  • Not a fatwa service · not a substitute for a living teacher
  • Not a grade of īmān or a certificate of scholarship
  • Not legal advice for courts, estates, or wills
  • Not a replacement for your local masjid, scholar, or community

How to use it with adab

  • Verify important matters with Qurʾān, Sunnah, and a trusted teacher
  • Follow your group’s guide for Ḥajj and ritual practice
  • Progress and notes stay on this device unless you copy them
  • Nothing is uploaded for “scoring” or institutional credit

Educational only — not a fatwa. Last reviewed Oct 2026 · Quran.com / Sunnah.com as reading aids.
