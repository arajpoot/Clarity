University charter

Purpose

Clarity University is an on-device educational path to organize Islamic learning with adab: Qur’an, authentic reports where cited, and practical character.

What it is not

  • Not a physical or accredited university
  • Not a fatwa service or court
  • Not a replacement for qualified teachers

Sources

Prefer Qur’an and ṣaḥīḥ reports with collection · number · grade. When the text does not settle a point, the site says so.
