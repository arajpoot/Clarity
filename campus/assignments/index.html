يَا أَيُّهَا الَّذِينَ آمَنُوا لِمَ تَقُولُونَ مَا لَا تَفْعَلُونَ
O you who believe, why do you say what you do not do?
Aṣ-Ṣaff 61:2

Educational reminder to align speech and action — not a personal ruling.

Assignments as ʿamal

Mirror, not scoreboard. Acceptance is with Allah.

Phase 1 · Foundation

Memorize or review Al-Ikhlāṣ with meaning. Write three names of Allah you will remember today.

Phase 2 · Pillars

Pray one ṣalāh today with full calm in rukūʿ and sujūd. Note one distraction you set aside.

Phase 3 · Iḥsān

One act of character from the sīrah (gentleness or honesty). Optional: five minutes slow tilāwah.

Phase 4 · Amānah

Share one authentic sentence (āyah or ṣaḥīḥ meaning) with sincerity — or remain silent if unsure of the source.
