Four learning phases

Aligned with Islam · Īmān · Iḥsān, then amānah of knowledge — in the spirit of classical progressive study (personally essential first).

Phase 1

Foundation of Īmān

Tawḥīd, pillars of faith, short sūrahs with meaning. Wake the heart with clarity.

 Phase 2

Pillars of practice

Ṭahārah, ṣalāh, overview of ṣawm and zakāh. Stability before volume.

 Phase 3

Path of iḥsān

Qur’an craft, sīrah as example, character, weekly review.

 Phase 4

Amānah of knowledge

Teach with wisdom. Guard sources. Leave beneficial knowledge.
