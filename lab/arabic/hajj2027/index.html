How to practice (lingua)

  1. Read along — see Arabic + transliteration + meaning.
  2. Listen — device TTS (Arabic voice if available); slow once.
  3. Match score — self-check that you said it (helper only).
  4. Filter by category: airport · market · food · hotel · help · places.

Talbiyah and rite-specific duʿāʾ belong with your teacher / official guides — not as “tourist Arabic” cards.
