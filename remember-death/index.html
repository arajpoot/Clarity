Remember death in Islam

Legacy path · Day 1 of 7 · ← Hub · Day 2 →

One sentence: remember death so that the next choice is softer and more honest.

Disclaimer. Educational. Not a fatwa. Not an invitation to despair or to neglect the living.

Last reviewed: September 2026 · Sunnah.com

Do this today

  • One quiet minute of remembrance
  • One small deed of obedience after it
  • Repair one sharp word if you can

What the sources say

The Prophet ﷺ encouraged frequent remembrance of the destroyer of pleasures (death). Confirm the exact wording with a teacher and read related chapters on Sunnah.com.

How schools differ

The virtue of remembrance is widely taught. Practices around visiting graves and speaking at them differ — follow local teaching.

Common mistakes

  • Using death-talk to scare children without hope
  • Leaving work and family “because we die anyway”
  • Skipping the one deed after the thought

FAQ

Does thinking of death reduce iman?

Done as the Sunnah intends, it should increase honesty and hope in Allah, not paralysis.
