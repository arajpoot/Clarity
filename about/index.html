About Clarity — purpose of this site

Clarity is a private, on-device-friendly learning space that helps Muslims and seekers walk a clear path: from first questions about faith, to steady practice, to responsible daʿwah.

It is built as a curriculum-oriented companion — not a social network, not a fatwa desk, and not a replacement for teachers or the local mosque.

Why Clarity exists

  • Reduce overwhelm by ordering knowledge into paths (Seeker → New Muslim → Daily → Daʿī).
  • Keep study practical: short lessons, modules you can open, notes and reflection tools.
  • Respect privacy: vault and notes are designed for on-device use where possible.
  • Serve daʿwah with adab: wisdom, accuracy, and caution with weak reports.

Learning paths (backbone)

Seeker — Junior
Who is Allah, īmān map, pillars overview, the Messenger ﷺ, adab, hope in the Hereafter.
New Muslim — Junior high
Purification, prayer outline, ḥalāl daily map, yearly pillars overview, Seerah into action.
Daily — High school
Tajweed practice, tafseer/deepen tools, weekly review, enrichment (calligraphy, Asmāʾ).
Daʿī — University-style track
Usūl of daʿwah, prophetic method, content craft, caution with Isrāʾīliyyāt, portfolio capstone.

What you will find

  • Live Qur’an command-verses and reflection cards
  • Tajweed studio, notes, meme reminders (scenery-safe)
  • Grave-path and end-of-life education topics (linked below)
  • Static guides for janazah, wasiyyah, and related community needs

What Clarity is not

  • Not a substitute for qualified scholarship or personal fatwa
  • Not medical, legal, or financial advice
  • Not a place that claims ijāzah or university credit

Read the Charter for principles we try to uphold.
