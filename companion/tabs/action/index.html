Guarding the tongue

Speech is recorded. Silence can be charity; harmful words can sink deeds.

Sunnah Educational only

The Prophet ﷺ said that whoever believes in Allah and the Last Day should speak good or remain silent (Bukhārī & Muslim). He also linked ruin of people to their tongues in various authentic warnings — verify on Sunnah.com.

Guardrails

  • Before speaking: is it true? is it necessary? is it kind enough?
  • Backbiting, mockery, and exposing faults without need — stop and seek tawbah.
  • Online posts are speech: same scale, wider audience.

Today, pause three seconds before any reply that feels sharp.

Protect your new start

Do not let arguments about Islam harden your tongue. Learn more than you debate.

◎

Family first

The tongue’s test is often at home. Soft speech there outweighs public eloquence.

◎

Public speech is heavier

If you call to Allah, edit twice: once for accuracy, once for adab. Delete what only serves the ego.

Small deeds, steady days

The most beloved deeds to Allah are those done consistently, even if small.

Bukhārī Muslim Educational only

Reported in Ṣaḥīḥ al-Bukhārī and Ṣaḥīḥ Muslim — verify full chains/wording on Sunnah.com. Spiritual life is built by return, not by rare intensity alone.

Three strands

  • Ṣabr — patience in obedience, in avoiding harm, and in trial.
  • Wird — a fixed portion of dhikr or Qurʾān tied to real times (after ṣalāh, morning/evening).
  • Istiqāmah — when you slip, renew and continue; do not burn the road behind you.

Yā Muqallib al-qulūb, thabbit qalbī ʿalā dīnik — O Turner of hearts, make my heart firm upon Your religion.

Post-prayer dhikr (among authentic forms): Subḥānallāh 33, al-ḥamdu lillāh 33, Allāhu akbar 33 (variants in Muslim). Tawakkul: take means, then rely on Allah.

Building on Pillars — stability before volume

Outer worship and clear practice first. Complexity comes after the foundations stand.

Qurʾān Sunnah Educational only

“And establish prayer and give zakāh and obey the Messenger — that you may receive mercy.” — Qurʾān 24:56 (meaning)

The five prayers are the daily spine. The Prophet ﷺ taught that the first matter judged on the Day of Resurrection is the prayer (reports in the Sunan collections — verify on Sunnah.com).

Stability sequence

  1. Wuḍūʾ & ṣalāh — learn structure slowly; Arabic grows with use.
  2. On-time prayer — protect the five before stacking large optional loads.
  3. Ḥalāl / ḥarām basics — food, speech, dealings; ask a teacher when unsure.
  4. Then optional wird, long nawāfil, and advanced study.

🔥 Warnings of the Fire< deeds Allah linked to severe punishment

This is a reminder, not a fatwa. The Prophet ﷺ named seven destructive sins (al-mūbiqāt) in a report agreed upon by al-Bukhārī and Muslim. Other verses and ṣaḥīḥ reports also attach a warning of the Fire to specific crimes. A believer who dies upon unrepented shirk is not forgiven; other major sins are under Allah’s will — He may punish or pardon — and sincere tawbah is accepted before death. Verify personal cases with a trusted scholar.

Core ḥadīth: Abū Hurayrah reported that the Messenger of Allah ﷺ said: “Avoid the seven destructive sins.” They asked, “What are they, O Messenger of Allah?” He said: “Associating partners with Allah; sorcery; killing a soul Allah has forbidden except by right; consuming ribā; consuming an orphan’s wealth; fleeing the battlefield; and slandering chaste, believing women who are unaware.” — Ṣaḥīḥ al-Bukhārī 2766 / 6857, Ṣaḥīḥ Muslim 89.

How scholars describe tawbah (repentance)

Imām al-Nawawī (Riyāḍ al-Ṣāliḥīn) and the majority: tawbah is obligatory from every sin. If the sin is only between the servant and Allah, three conditions; if it violates a human right, a fourth is added.

1. Iqlaʿ
Stop the sin at once.
2. Nadam
Feel genuine remorse.
3. ʿAzm
Resolve never to return.
4. Ḥaqq al-ādamī
Return wealth, honour, or seek pardon if a person was wronged.

Window of acceptance: before the soul reaches the throat, and before the sun rises from the west (ḥadīth in Muslim and others). Allah says: “Say: O My servants who have transgressed against themselves, do not despair of the mercy of Allah. Indeed Allah forgives all sins. He is the Forgiving, the Merciful.” (al-Zumar 39:53). And: “…except those who repent, believe, and do righteous deeds — for them Allah will replace their evil deeds with good deeds.” (al-Furqān 25:70).

Also: Anas reported the qudsī ḥadīth: “O son of Ādam, were you to come to Me with an earthful of sins and then meet Me without having associated anything with Me, I would come to you with an earthful of pardon.” (al-Tirmidhī 3540; graded ḥasan by many, including al-Albānī). Ibn al-Qayyim: tawbah is not a phrase on the tongue only — it is leaving, regretting, and replacing harm with obedience.
