Disclaimer. Not legal advice. Laws change. This is a collision note, not a deed.

Do this today

  • Read the main wasiyyah page first
  • Book a lawyer who has written Muslim wills in Pakistan

What this page will not do

It will not generate a Pakistan will. It will not list stamp duty, court fees, or current statutes.

Collision in brief

Pakistan’s Muslim personal law and court practice interact with farāʾiḍ more directly than the UK, but families still fail on documentation, agricultural land, and unrecorded debts. A local advocate plus a mufti is the pair — not a foreign PDF.

Common mistakes

  • Downloading another country’s template
  • Assuming a mosque stamp replaces probate
  • Leaving digital accounts and pensions out of the civil document
