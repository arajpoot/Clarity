Governance contract

One page for the rules Clarity is bound by. This is a discovery document — it does not replace Companion, Yawm, University, Amānah, or Craft, and it does not change their navigation.

What Clarity is: a private, educational companion for learning and remembrance.

What Clarity is not: a fatwa desk, a court, a bank, a seminary, or a substitute for a living teacher you trust.

1. Core submission rules

These limits apply across the castle. Room apps may add stricter local rules; they may not weaken these.

  1. Arabic on top where Qur’ān text is shown. Translation is a bridge, not a replacement.
  2. No practice card without a citation the user can open (Qur’ān reference or collection · number).
  3. Do not say “the Sunnah is…” when you mean “this report in Bukhārī / Muslim …” — name the report.
  4. Do not mix ranks: Qur’ān, ṣaḥīḥ reports, and optional media must not look equal on the same visual tier.
  5. When two ṣaḥīḥ reports need fiqh to reconcile: show both and send the user to a scholar. Do not invent a third source called Clarity.
  6. Night and grave-facing content stays Qur’ān, ṣaḥīḥ, death-awareness, and istighfār — not entertainment sīrah.

2. Sources policy

Preferred primary libraries

  • Quran.com — Qur’ān text and translations
  • Sunnah.com — hadith collections with numbers

How reports should appear

  • Prefer collection · number · grade when a report is shown as evidence.
  • If a grade is not loaded: say so honestly and link the source page.
  • Do not present machine translation as an official tafsīr.

Full page: Sources policy.

3. Privacy & on-device data

  • Sensitive Amānah material is designed to stay on this device where implemented that way.
  • Clarity does not store your vault passphrase on Clarity servers.
  • Private trust notes are not published as public web pages.
  • Use a strong device passcode; back up anything important with a method you control.
  • Treat educational screens as learning aids — not court filings.

Full page: Privacy & on-device data. Product desk: Amānah.

5. Room-specific charters (summary)

Campus · University

  • Not a physical university or accredited degree.
  • Not affiliated with Al-Azhar, Madinah, IIUM, or any named institution.
  • Path markers are a study mirror — not a grade of īmān.
  • Not a substitute for a living teacher.

See the room’s Charter page and University.

Craft · Media daʿwah

  • Source discipline — scripture lines from section packs; never free-type as revelation.
  • Scenery only — nature, night, water, flowers, solid; no figurative people lists as “revelation art.”
  • Shariah scan — remote background URLs must pass the on-page scan before paint.
  • Educational stamp — exports carry “not a fatwa.”
  • Adab — post what you would stand by before Allah (cf. 16:125).

Workbench: Meme Studio.

Amānah · Trust desk

  • Desk notes need no unlock; deeper chambers use an optional on-device passphrase.
  • Not a fatwa, not a bank, not a court will, not an inheritance order.
  • Civil law may also apply in the user’s country — Clarity does not replace it.

Companion · Pathways

  • Four doors (Seeker → New Muslim → Daily Muslim → Aspiring Daʿī) are educational tracks, not ranks of īmān.
  • Ordered unlock / short quiz where implemented is a study ladder, not a certificate.
  • Section visibility should match the active path — tools must not silently reopen locked desks.

6. What “done” looks like

A practicing Muslim can open Clarity at fajr and mainly see:

  • Hear this — Qur’ān / ṣaḥīḥ with a path to verify.
  • Obey this — one clear educational action, not a private ruling.
  • Send it ahead — deeds and notes that stay on the device unless exported.

They can study death, janāzah outlines, and wasiyyah education without mistaking the screen for a scholar or a court.

They never need a madhhab picker to use the home screen.

7. Change control

  • This page consolidates policy. Room apps keep their own code and nav.
  • Stricter room rules are allowed; weaker ones are not.
  • If a feature cannot name its source or keep private data local, it does not ship under Clarity’s name.
