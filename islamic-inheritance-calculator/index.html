Islamic inheritance calculator — Hanafi-leaning notes

Clarity’s in-app tool is a study aid. Real estates need a qualified scholar plus a lawyer in your country.

Order of payout

  1. Funeral costs (reasonable).
  2. Debts.
  3. Wasiyyah up to one-third of what remains.
  4. Faraʾid shares on the residue.

Worked example A — wife and two sons (no parents)

Residue after debts and wasiyyah: 100. Wife takes 1/8 = 12.5. Sons share the rest equally: 43.75 each. (Daughters would take with 2:1 male-to-female among children when sons exist.)

Worked example B — only a daughter and a father

Hanafi outline: daughter often takes 1/2 as a Qur’anic share; father takes 1/6 + residue as ʿaṣabah in many common trees. Confirm ʿawl / radd if the fractions overflow or fall short.

ʿAwl and radd

If shares exceed the whole, they are scaled down (ʿawl). If they fall short and there is no residuary, remainder may return to those entitled (radd), with well-known Hanafi exceptions for a spouse in some cases.
