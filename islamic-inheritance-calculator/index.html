What the sources say

Allah details shares for children, parents, spouses, and others in Sūrat al-Nisāʾ. Read on quran.com/4/11.

Worked example (simplified)

A man dies leaving a wife, two sons, no parents. After funeral and debts: wife receives 1/8 (because there are children). The remainder is divided between the two sons equally in this sketch. Real families add daughters, siblings, grandparents — stop here and ask.

How schools differ

Most of the Qur’anic fractions are shared. Differences appear in ʿawl, radd, and some distant relatives. Hanafi court practice is not automatically UK or Pakistani civil practice.

Common mistakes

  • Giving the eldest son “everything because he is the man of the house”
  • Cutting a daughter out by family custom
  • Using this page as an estate account
