Nur Path

Journey · four colleges

One open module at a time. Checkmark unlocks the next. Phase quiz opens the following college. Records stay on this device.

Phase 1 · College of Foundations (Seeker)

  • Tawhid — Allah is One; six pillars of iman (map)
  • Qur'an — adab, Al-Fatiha, gentle Ulum map
  • Hadith — why we follow the Messenger ﷺ; authentic seed; chain intro
  • Fiqh of worship — taharah, five prayers (beginner map)

Phase 2 · College of Practice (New Muslim)

  • Confidence after shahadah — capacity, where to ask
  • Taharah in practice — wudu map, what breaks wudu
  • Salah path — times, rak'ahs, khushu
  • Daily adhkar and manners with people

Phase 3 · College of Consistency (Daily)

  • Tajweed foundations — makharij awareness, one rule applied
  • Daily wird — sustainable portion, tadabbur seed
  • Strengthening salah — rawatib, congregation
  • Ramadan purpose & zakat as purification (overview)

Phase 4 · College of Transmission (Daʿi)

  • Ethics of the caller — intention, humility, knowledge before speech
  • Source discipline — primary sources, citing with context
  • Audience & wisdom — seeker care, disagreement with adab
  • Service over platform — never “graduate” from learning
