Clarity Charter

This charter states how Clarity aims to serve learners. It is a living commitment, not a legal contract.

1. Purpose

Clarity exists to support Islamic learning and responsible daʿwah through ordered paths, practical modules, and privacy-respecting tools — for the sake of Allah.

2. Sources & humility

  • Primary references: the Qur’an and authentic Sunnah, as understood through reliable scholarship.
  • We prefer clarity over controversy; when schools of law differ, we encourage asking a local teacher.
  • Weak or unverified stories (including careless Isrāʾīliyyāt) must not be taught as certain dīn.

3. Curriculum backbone

  • Seeker (junior): foundations of belief and hope.
  • New Muslim (junior high): purification, prayer, daily ḥalāl map.
  • Daily (high school): tajweed, meaning, weekly accountability.
  • Daʿī (university-style): method, ethics, content craft, portfolio — without claiming formal certification.

4. Adab of invitation

  • Invite with wisdom and good instruction (Qur’an 16:125).
  • No compulsion in religion; no mockery of people.
  • Media and memes: scenery and text only — no prophet likeness, no using muṣḥaf as decoration.

5. Privacy & trust (amānah)

  • Notes and vault features are intended for user-controlled, on-device storage where implemented.
  • We do not ask for belief tests to use the educational paths.
  • Users should not paste secrets they cannot afford to lose on any web device.

6. Limits

  • Educational reflection only — not a fatwa service.
  • End-of-life and inheritance pages are general education; local law and qualified scholars govern real cases.
  • External links are for learning; Clarity does not control third-party sites.

7. Improvement

Paths, activities, and static guides will improve over time. Errors should be corrected with humility. Feedback through community channels is welcome when offered with adab.
