University Charter

Clarity University is the structured desk beside Companion. Progress stays on this device. Primary texts stay with the ummah’s free libraries (Qurʾān · Sunnah).

Who it is for

  • Seekers and new Muslims who want one open door at a time.
  • Families who want private notes and literacy tools — not cloud īmān scores.
  • Anyone practising small Lab skills after a short lesson.

What it never does

  1. We do not issue fatwa or court advice.
  2. We do not sell a madhhab or replace a living teacher.
  3. We do not treat weak reports as practice.
  4. We do not store your vault passphrase on our servers (there are none for that).
  5. A streak or checklist is not a witness; acceptance is with Allah.

How phases work

  1. Seeker — belief foundations; one module open; spark quiz unlocks the next.
  2. Practice — New Muslim path after the Junior phase quiz.
  3. Consistency — steady craft and review.
  4. Transmission — share with adab and sources, within clear limits.

After a module: understand → one small practice → Lab chip → optional Library pointer.

Companion vs University

  • Companion — daily banner, ṣalāh, reminder path, on-device tools on the home page.
  • University — Map, Desk rooms, Lab, Library, Vault on campus routes.

This charter governs the University path only.

Primary sources

Deep reading opens on trusted free sites (for example Quran.com, Sunnah.com). Campus points; it does not replace the Book or the Sunnah.

Not affiliated with Al-Azhar, Madinah University, or any seminary. Educational only — verify with Qurʾān, Sunnah, and a local scholar.
