University Charter

Structured learning beside Companion. Progress stays on this device. Primary texts stay with the ummah’s free libraries. Educational only — not a fatwa service.

1 · Admit 2 · Map 3 · Study 4 · Practise
Who it is for
  • Seekers and new Muslims who want one open door at a time.
  • Families who want private notes — not cloud īmān scores.
  • Anyone practising small Lab skills after a short lesson.
What it never does
  1. We do not issue fatwa or court advice.
  2. We do not sell a madhhab or replace a living teacher.
  3. We do not treat weak reports as practice.
  4. We do not store your passphrase or ledger on our servers.
  5. A streak or checklist is not a witness; acceptance is with Allah.
How phases work
  1. Seeker — belief foundations; one module open.
  2. Practice — skills after the Seeker phase quiz.
  3. Consistency — steady craft and review.
  4. Transmission — share with adab and clear sources.

After each module: understand → one practice → Lab → optional Library.
