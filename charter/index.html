Educational Charter

بسم الله الرحمن الرحيم

Not a university degree. Not a fatwa service. Not affiliated with Al-Azhar, the Islamic University of Madinah, or any state institution.

Clarity is a local-first educational companion. Progress stays in your browser. For personal rulings, ask a qualified local teacher.

Method we borrow (inspiration only)

Classical Islamic study often moves in order: creed and revelation, then worship, then steady practice, then responsible transmission. Great centers historically separated preparatory stages from specialization (for example: theology, jurisprudence, Qur’an sciences, hadith literacy, and daʿwah ethics). We mirror that sequence as four gentle riwāq (study halls) — not their curriculum, ranks, or certificates.

Four riwāq on Campus

  1. Foundations — Tawhid, Qur’an literacy, hadith introduction, pillars map
  2. Practice — Purification, salah confidence, everyday worship map
  3. Consistency — Tajweed habit, wird, stronger daily discipline
  4. Transmission — Adab of sharing, source honesty, service without coercion

Rules of the desk

  • One module open at a time; mark complete before the next unlocks
  • Prefer Qur’an.com and graded hadith tools; verify before you share
  • No claim of ijazah, ʿalim status, or institutional endorsement
  • Lab is craft practice; Vault is private on-device notes

Staff and faculty labels

Titles such as Dean, Tutor, Registrar, or Security are educational UI roles that guide the interface. They are not academic appointments.
