Amana Vault

Educational only · not a fatwa · not legal advice

Privacy first. Vault contents are never uploaded and are not searchable on Google. This page only explains the tool so people can find it.

What it is

Amana Vault holds private notes for estate readiness: who you leave behind, debts and trusts to settle, wasiyyah thoughts, family outline, and farāʾiḍ study helpers. It is a trust (amānah) between you and those who may need this after you — not a court filing and not a fatwa.

How privacy works

  • Passphrase stays offline — Clarity does not store or recover it
  • Data is local to your browser / device
  • Export encrypted backups you control
  • Google and other crawlers cannot read your vault entries

What you can open in the app

  • Unlock gate and private notes
  • Family tree / pedigree workspace
  • Member registry and print sheets
  • Related farāʾiḍ study tools (educational)

Open the app, choose the Amana Vault tab, and unlock with your passphrase.
